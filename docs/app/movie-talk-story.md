## 1. What a "movie talk" is

A TPRS (Teaching Proficiency through Reading and Storytelling) technique:
a short silent video, shown in pieces, with the facilitator pausing it to
narrate and ask simple questions in the target language — comprehension
practice built around a story rather than a drill. No actual video exists
yet; this document is structure and placeholder characters only, not
production.

## 3. Structure, if/when this becomes real

- One short story arc per week or per block, tied to that week's theme
  and vocabulary (matching how `weekly_content` already works)
- Paused at natural points for facilitator narration and simple
  comprehension questions
- No fast cuts, minimal on-screen text — silent-film-adjacent, so
  language load stays low
- Characters age-appropriate and dignity-forward throughout — the same
  brand voice rule that applies everywhere else ("students," never
  "patients")
