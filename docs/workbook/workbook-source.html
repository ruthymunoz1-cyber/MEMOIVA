Welcome

This workbook is yours.

Welcome to MEMOIVA Circle — a warm, weekly program to keep your mind active and your confidence growing. This workbook travels with you through all eight weeks of your program: new vocabulary each week, a place for your own notes, and a calming activity you can return to anytime between sessions.

Bring it to every session. Write in it. Dog-ear the pages. It's meant to be used, not kept pristine.

Everything in this workbook is also available in the MEMOIVA Circle app, included with your enrollment — use whichever one feels easiest for you, or both.

MEMOIVA Circle is inspired by peer-reviewed research on bilingualism and cognitive health. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any medical condition. The assessments used in MEMOIVA Circle are educational tools, not clinical diagnostic instruments. If you have concerns about your cognitive health, please consult a qualified healthcare provider.
